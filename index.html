How it's calculated: monthly amortisation over the remaining term. Interest accrues monthly at rate ÷ 12. For the tracker, each base-rate change takes effect from the start of the chosen month and the monthly payment is recalculated on the outstanding balance over the remaining months (as UK lenders do). The fixed payment stays constant throughout the comparison period. The break-even base rate is the constant base rate at which both options pay the same interest over the comparison period. The base rate can't go below 0%. This is an illustration only, not financial advice. Real products may have collars, fees, early repayment charges, reversion to a standard variable rate, and slightly different interest calculation methods.
